How This Works

In traditional quick-commerce, fulfilling small orders as separate trips incurs high delivery cost. Fastora's Smart Delivery Economics groups orders from the same neighbourhood into scheduled time slots. Because multiple customer orders are fulfilled in one continuous delivery route, the delivery cost drops, allowing us to pass discounts directly to customers.
